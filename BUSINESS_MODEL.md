# Fiple Business Model & Revenue Streams

**Complete Revenue Strategy for Sustainable Growth**

---

## Executive Summary

Fiple operates on a **multi-stream revenue model** combining:
1. Subscription revenue (Creator+ plans)
2. Advertising (display, sponsored, native)
3. Transaction fees (withdrawals, payments)
4. Business promotions (promoted posts, campaigns)
5. Marketplace commission (Skill Exchange)
6. Virtual goods (Fiple Points, gifts)
7. Premium features (verification badges, analytics)
8. Enterprise solutions (campus partnerships)

**Target:** ₦100M+ monthly revenue at 500K active users

## Revenue Stream 2: ADVERTISING (40% of Revenue)

### A. Ad Formats

#### 1. **Display Ads** (Banner Ads)
```
Placement: Feed, between posts (every 5 posts)
Size: 320x50 (mobile banner), 728x90 (desktop)
Format: Image, GIF, HTML5
Pricing: CPM (Cost Per Mille)
Rate: ₦500-2,000 CPM
Target: Free users only
```

#### 2. **Native Ads** (Sponsored Posts)
```
Placement: Appears in feed like regular posts
Format: Image + caption + "Sponsored" label
Engagement: Likes, comments, shares
Pricing: CPC (Cost Per Click) or CPM
Rate: ₦50-200 per click, ₦1,000-5,000 CPM
Target: All users
```

#### 3. **Video Ads** (Pre-roll, In-stream)
```
Placement: Before reels, between stories
Duration: 5-15 seconds (skippable after 5s)
Format: MP4, MOV
Pricing: CPV (Cost Per View)
Rate: ₦20-100 per view
Target: Free users
```

#### 4. **Story Ads** (Full-screen Immersive)
```
Placement: Between user stories
Duration: 5-10 seconds
Format: Vertical video, image
Pricing: CPM or CPC
Rate: ₦2,000-8,000 CPM
Target: High engagement format
```

#### 5. **Campus Hub Ads** (Targeted to Students)
```
Placement: Campus hub pages
Targeting: By university, course, year
Format: Banner, native, video
Pricing: CPM + targeting premium
Rate: ₦3,000-10,000 CPM
Target: Student-focused brands
```

#### 6. **Promoted Posts** (Business Accounts)
```
Placement: Boosted to top of feed
Duration: 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days
Targeting: Location, age, interests, campus
Pricing: Budget-based (₦5,000 minimum)
Rate: CPC or CPM
Target: Local businesses, brands
```

### C. Ad Pricing Models

| Model | Description | Best For | Rate |
|-------|-------------|----------|------|
| **CPM** | Cost per 1,000 impressions | Brand awareness | ₦500-10,000 |
| **CPC** | Cost per click | Traffic, leads | ₦50-500 |
| **CPV** | Cost per video view | Video campaigns | ₦20-200 |
| **CPA** | Cost per action (install, signup) | Conversions | ₦500-5,000 |
| **CPL** | Cost per lead | Lead generation | ₦1,000-10,000 |

### D. Ad Revenue Projections (100K Users)

```
Assumptions:
- 90,000 free users (see ads)
- Average 20 ad impressions per user per day
- 30% ad fill rate
- Average CPM: ₦1,000

Daily Impressions: 90,000 × 20 × 0.3 = 540,000
Daily Revenue: (540,000 ÷ 1,000) × ₦1,000 = ₦540,000
Monthly Revenue: ₦540,000 × 30 = ₦16,200,000

Plus:
- Promoted posts: ~₦5,000,000/month
- Video ads: ~₦3,000,000/month
- Campus hub ads: ~₦2,000,000/month

Total Ad Revenue: ₦26,200,000/month (~$34,000)
```

## RISK MITIGATION

### Revenue Risks
1. **Ad blocker usage** → Mobile-first (lower ad blocker penetration)
2. **Low subscription conversion** → Freemium model ensures revenue
3. **High reward costs** → Capped by user growth requirements
4. **Competition** → Unique cultural focus + rewards differentiate

### Mitigation Strategies
- Diversify revenue (8 streams)
- Start conservative, scale ads gradually
- A/B test pricing
- Monitor unit economics closely
