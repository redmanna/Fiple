# Privacy Policy for Fiple

**Last Updated:** November 25, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Fiple ("we," "our," or "us"). Fiple is Nigeria's premier campus social commerce platform that connects students through content creation, social networking, marketplace, skill exchange, and rewards.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We collect information that you provide directly to us when you:
- Create an account (email, username, full name, phone number)
- Complete your profile (profile picture, bio, campus affiliation)
- Make purchases or transactions (delivery address, payment information via Paystack)
- Contact customer support
- Participate in surveys or promotions

### Automatically Collected Information
When you use Fiple, we automatically collect:
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** App features used, time spent, interactions, search queries
- **Location Data:** Approximate location (with your permission) to show local content and campus hubs
- **Camera & Photos:** When you create posts or reels (only with your permission)
- **Microphone:** When you record audio for reels (only with your permission)

### User-Generated Content
We collect content you create and share on Fiple:
- Posts, comments, messages, reviews
- Photos, videos, live streams
- Products listed for sale
- Affiliate partnerships and promotions

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- **Provide Services:** Create and manage your account, process transactions, enable social features
- **Personalization:** Show relevant content, products, and campus-specific information
- **Communication:** Send notifications about messages, rewards, followers, and activity updates
- **Safety & Security:** Detect fraud, prevent abuse, enforce terms of service
- **Analytics:** Understand usage patterns and improve the app
- **Marketing:** Send promotional offers (you can opt out anytime)
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with applicable laws and regulations

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share your information in the following circumstances:

### With Other Users
- Your public profile information (username, profile picture, bio)
- Content you post publicly (posts, comments, reviews)
- Your merchant/vendor information if you sell products

### With Service Providers
- **Paystack:** Payment processing (they have their own privacy policy)
- **Cloud Hosting:** Expo, AWS, or similar services for app infrastructure
- **Analytics:** To understand app usage and improve services
- **Customer Support:** To respond to your inquiries

### For Legal Reasons
We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe disclosure is necessary to:
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect and defend our rights or property
- Prevent fraud or illegal activity
- Protect the safety of users or the public

### Business Transfers
If Fiple is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:
- **Encryption:** Sensitive data like authentication tokens are encrypted on your device
- **Secure Storage:** We use expo-secure-store for encrypted local storage
- **HTTPS:** All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS
- **Access Controls:** Limited access to personal information by employees

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Information
You can update your account information at any time through the app settings.

### Location Data
You can disable location access through your device settings at any time.

### Camera, Photos, and Microphone
You can revoke access to camera, photos, and microphone through your device settings.

### Push Notifications
You can disable push notifications through your device settings or in-app settings.

### Marketing Communications
You can opt out of promotional emails by clicking "unsubscribe" or through app settings.

### Data Access and Deletion
You have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and data
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@fiple.app.

### Data Retention
We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request deletion at any time, after which we will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except as required by law.

## Children's Privacy

Fiple is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at privacy@fiple.app.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of Nigeria. By using Fiple, you consent to the transfer of information to countries outside of your country of residence, which may have different data protection laws.

## Third-Party Links
